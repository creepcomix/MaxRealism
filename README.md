### What This Is
MaxRealism is my personal take on realistic texture packs. No compromises, no nostalgia—just photorealistic textures built to make Minecraft look like something you'd actually want to photograph. Every block has been rebuilt from scratch with detail that actually matters.

### The Details That Matter
All textures support labPBR with POM (Parallax Occlusion Mapping), so surfaces have real depth and dimension. Deepslate looks like stone you can feel. Wood grain catches light. Sand shifts under your feet.

### Why Make This?
I wanted a realistic pack where I could push the detail as far as it'd go—no holding back. MaxRealism is that pack.
